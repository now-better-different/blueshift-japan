No. AI compresses the slow part — collecting and modeling hundreds of Japanese and English sources. The AI never writes your recommendations; it buys back the hours so a 20-year trader can get them right.

How is my confidential information handled?

Confidential details are processed only in enterprise API environments with zero-data-retention — your inputs are not stored after the request and are never used to train AI models. We're glad to sign your NDA first, and we can work from anonymized or redacted inputs. See our Privacy Policy for detail.

Who actually writes the report?

The founder, Tomoaki Nakao — personally. No junior analysts. He spent 20 years at Sumitomo Corporation across trade, cross-border investment, and business development. You can verify his background on LinkedIn.

Every engagement includes a revision round after you've read it — Starter and Standard each include 1 round; Premium includes 2. That's your chance to sharpen the focus once the report is in front of you. The goal isn't a thick deck — it's a clear go / no-go answer you can act on.

Starter and Standard are paid in full upfront; Premium is split 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery. Your report is delivered in 5–15 business days from receipt of your completed questionnaire. Everything runs over email, on your schedule and timezone. If you upgrade to a higher plan within 90 days of delivery, you pay only the difference.
